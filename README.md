# The World in One Day — Slot 2 "The Full Day"

Entry for the make.fun **news-image-20261119** bounty: the whole world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 picture — 36 stories, 6 continents, one day.

Slot 2 was built after the full day was over, so its heroes are the day's resolved outcomes, not the morning schedule: the ICJ's 9–6 genocide verdict against Myanmar, the Trump–Xi–Putin peace declaration signed in Shenzhen, and GTA VI's $2.36bn opening day (Sensor Tower estimate, labelled as such). Nine afternoon stories slot 1 lacked are in: the third Kerch Bridge strike, the Houthi missile on Riyadh's PIF Tower, Wall Street's fifth straight fall, the end of the first Gaza ground raid, the AI cyberattack on the European Commission, Somalia expelling the UAE ambassador, Denmark's 109–0 US–Greenland vote, the South Korea ex-PM retrial, and the US Senate's Iran war-powers vote.

Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Thursday 19 November 2026 (UTC). Follow-ups are labelled with their date in the DATE CHECK box, which also lists what was deliberately left out because it was first reported on 17–18 November.
